# Grounding

- **Report only what you saw this run.** A claim from a page carries its URL, the date you read it, and a verbatim quote.
- **Found or inferred:** mark every line **found** (seen on a page or in the source) or **inferred** (your deduction from what was seen).
- **A quote evidences the specific claim.** It is copied from the payload you read. Page furniture (navigation, banners, headings, calls to action) is verbatim and proves nothing.
- **Numbers are quotes:** stars, prices, dates and thresholds arrive with their source line, or as "not found". A figure you derive is recomputed, or marked uncheckable.
- **Memory fills no gap.** A page you could not fetch is *unreachable*, with the error.
- **A negative is earned.** "Nobody does X" claims a complete view: say what you searched, list every candidate and place each, or report "presence could not be verified". A partial read (JS-rendered, gated, blocked, truncated, paywalled) never supports a negative.
- Every URL in your final message is complete.
